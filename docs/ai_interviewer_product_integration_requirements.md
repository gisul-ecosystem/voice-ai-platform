# AI Interviewer Product Integration Requirements

## Purpose

The AI Interviewer is a centrally deployed, product-neutral service consumed by
Aaptor, Kanonkode, and future products. Each consuming product owns its branded
user experience while this platform owns interview execution, real-time media,
lifecycle state, transcripts, and scoring.

## Deployment boundary

- The AI Interviewer backend, voice-agent workers, and LiveKit deployment are
  operated as one shared platform.
- LiveKit credentials remain exclusively in the AI Interviewer server
  environment.
- Product backends authenticate to the platform API. Raw LiveKit credentials
  are never shared with product frontends or backends.
- The existing `apps/voice-frontend` application is only a neutral development,
  testing, and reference client. It is not the production Aaptor or Kanonkode
  frontend.

## Product responsibilities

Aaptor, Kanonkode, or another integrating product owns:

- Recruiter/admin interview creation and scheduling screens.
- Candidate and administrator-facing branding and navigation.
- Branded invitation, reminder, reschedule, and cancellation emails.
- The candidate interview page.
- The administrator live-monitoring page.
- Rescheduling and cancellation workflows.
- Human review and presentation of scorecards and candidate feedback.
- Authentication of its own candidates, recruiters, and administrators.

## AI Interviewer platform responsibilities

This platform owns:

- Product-authenticated interview management and execution APIs.
- Secure invitation references and candidate join validation.
- Join-window, cancellation, attempt, and idempotency enforcement.
- LiveKit room creation and explicit AI worker dispatch.
- Short-lived candidate and listen-only monitor tokens.
- AI interviewer conversation execution.
- Authoritative session lifecycle reconciliation.
- Durable interview state, final transcripts, and audit records.
- Scorecard and feedback generation.
- Signed, idempotent lifecycle and result webhooks for consuming products.
- Retention, access control, observability, and failure recovery.

## End-to-end product flow

1. A recruiter creates and schedules an interview in the product UI.
2. The product backend creates the interview through the AI Interviewer API,
   supplying its external interview reference and required interview data.
3. The platform returns an opaque platform interview and invitation reference.
4. The product sends a branded email containing a URL on the product's domain.
5. The candidate opens that product URL and completes preview and consent.
6. The product backend exchanges the invitation reference through the platform
   join API.
7. The platform validates the interview and returns a short-lived candidate
   token and the AI Interviewer LiveKit URL.
8. The candidate and AI worker join the same AI Interviewer LiveKit room.
9. An authorized product administrator requests a listen-only monitor token.
10. The product monitoring UI connects to the same room and subscribes to the
    candidate and interviewer media.
11. The platform sends signed lifecycle and result events to the product and
    exposes status, transcript, scorecard, and feedback APIs.
12. The product presents those results through its own branded interface.

Kanonkode and future products use the same contract with their own branding,
email delivery, authentication, and user interfaces.

## LiveKit constraint

LiveKit rooms do not span independent LiveKit deployments. Aaptor's existing
LiveKit server cannot natively monitor an interview room hosted by the AI
Interviewer LiveKit server.

For AI interviews, the Aaptor or Kanonkode frontend must connect to the AI
Interviewer LiveKit URL using a platform-issued room token:

- Candidate token: room-specific and limited to required candidate media.
- Monitor token: room-specific, short-lived, subscribe-only, with audio, video,
  and data publishing disabled.
- Revocation must remove the monitor participant and create an audit event.

A product's existing LiveKit deployment can continue serving its unrelated
proctoring or communication workflows.

## Integration identity

Every request must identify and authenticate the integrating product. The
minimum integration contract requires:

- `product_id`, such as `aaptor` or `kanonkode`.
- `external_interview_id`, generated by the consuming product.
- A server-side product credential or signed service token.
- A registered webhook destination and signing secret.
- Registered candidate and administrator application origins.

An organization or tenant identifier is required only when a consuming product
serves multiple organizations whose data and administrator access must be
isolated. It is not a LiveKit requirement.

## Required platform contracts

The platform must ultimately expose contracts for:

- Creating, rescheduling, cancelling, and reading interviews.
- Non-consuming invitation preview and consent.
- Idempotent candidate joining.
- Session status and transcript retrieval.
- Listen-only monitor access, refresh, and revocation.
- Scorecard retrieval, retry, human review, and feedback release.
- Signed lifecycle, completion, and result webhooks.

All mutating APIs must support idempotency. Product identifiers and external
references must be scoped and uniquely constrained so one product cannot read
or mutate another product's interviews.

## Security requirements

- No provider API key or LiveKit API secret may be sent to a browser.
- Invitation URLs must contain only opaque, expiring, single-use references.
- A product service credential authenticates the calling application but does
  not by itself authorize a human administrator action.
- Monitor access requires product authorization, the appropriate human scope,
  recorded candidate disclosure/consent, and append-only auditing.
- Webhooks must be signed, timestamped, replay-protected, and idempotent.
- Candidate context, transcripts, recordings, and scorecards must follow the
  configured retention and data-egress policy.

## Current repository status

The repository currently provides basic interview context creation, invitation
signing, LiveKit session creation, AI worker dispatch, conversation execution,
turn persistence, and initial scorecard job creation.

It does not yet provide the complete product integration described above,
including scheduled interview aggregates, product integration authentication,
join-window enforcement, product webhooks, listen-only monitor APIs,
authoritative LiveKit webhook reconciliation, and completed scoring/results.

Implementation should proceed incrementally against this requirements document.
Branded Aaptor and Kanonkode frontends remain in their separate repositories.
